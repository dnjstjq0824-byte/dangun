# BN NEAR — 23. Privacy & Location Information Policy

**Order ID:** BN-NEAR-MASTER-001  
**Compliance Standards:** Global GDPR, South Korea Location Information Protection Act, California CCPA/CPRA  

---

## 1. Principles of Location Data Processing
1. **Explicit Consent:** Location access is requested exclusively after explaining the explicit purpose (verifying neighborhood residency). Users may freely refuse and manually browse neighborhoods in read-only mode.
2. **Ephemeral In-Memory Verification:** Precise GPS coordinates (latitude, longitude, altitude) are evaluated in-memory against administrative polygon boundaries and are NEVER written to persistent relational storage or log files.
3. **Public Coordinate Approximation:** When displaying an item or community post, the platform displays ONLY the neighborhood administrative name (e.g. "Cheongna-dong") or a randomized centroid offset (> 500 meters from any actual residence).
4. **No Continuous Tracking:** The platform does NOT record background location breadcrumbs or continuous movement tracking.
5. **Right to Erasure:** Users can revoke neighborhood associations and delete their account and associated metadata at any time.
