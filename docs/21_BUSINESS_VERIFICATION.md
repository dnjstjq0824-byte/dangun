# BN NEAR — 21. Local Business Verification Specification

**Order ID:** BN-NEAR-MASTER-001  
**Module:** `services/api/internal/business`  

---

## 1. Local Merchant Profiles
Local stores, neighborhood cafes, and domestic trade services can establish a verified business profile:
- **Core Attributes:** Business legal/trade name, category (e.g. Dining, Craft/Repair, Fitness, Groceries), physical storefront address, operating hours per weekday, verified phone contact, photo gallery.
- **Store Announcements Feed:** Merchants can publish local neighborhood updates, seasonal promotions, and community events directly to local residents.

---

## 2. Business Verification Protocol
To prevent deceptive commercial marketing and protect consumers:
1. **Application:** Merchant registers business details and submits verification proof (business registration certificate or storefront photo).
2. **Review:** Operational administrator reviews document validity and physical location alignment against neighborhood boundary.
3. **Badge Issuance:** Upon administrative approval, the merchant profile receives the "Verified Local Business" badge.
4. **Differentiation:** Business posts are clearly distinguished from regular resident discussions with a "Local Merchant" banner to preserve organic community trust.
