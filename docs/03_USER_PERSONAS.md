**Order ID:** BN-NEAR-MASTER-001  

---

## 1. Primary Personas

### Persona A: Local Buyer (e.g., Min-ji, 29, Seoul / Cheongna)
- **Goal:** Find quality baby gear, home appliances, or books within walking distance at honest prices.
- **Pain Points:** Fear of scams, awkward communication, dealing with shipping delays, unreliable sellers.
- **Key Features Used:** Neighborhood verification badge, distance slider, price offers, in-app chat, safe meeting scheduler.

### Persona B: Decluttering Seller (e.g., David, 38, Incheon / Songdo)
- **Goal:** Quickly sell unused tech gadgets and furniture to trustworthy neighbors without packaging/shipping overhead.
- **Pain Points:** Lowballers, no-shows, delayed reservations, ghosting after agreeing to buy.
- **Key Features Used:** Fast 60-second sell form, buyer verification check, reservation collision lock, one-tap mark-sold.

### Persona C: Community Inquirer (e.g., Sarah, 34, New Resident)
- **Goal:** Ask for reliable pediatrician recommendations, report a lost pet, or find local running buddies.
- **Pain Points:** Spam-filled open social forums, noise from non-residents, lack of local relevance.
- **Key Features Used:** Verified-resident-only community boards, category tags, threaded replies.

### Persona D: Small Merchant / Local Cafe Owner (e.g., Master Kim, 46)
- **Goal:** Promote weekly bakery specials or repair services to immediate residential blocks.
- **Pain Points:** Prohibitive advertising costs on global search giants, inability to reach real local residents.
- **Key Features Used:** Local business profile, store hours, business announcements feed.

### Persona E: Trust & Safety Moderator (Internal Operations)
- **Goal:** Rapidly investigate reported listings, toxic chats, or spam bots, enforcing terms fairly with audit logging.
- **Pain Points:** Lack of contextual evidence, privacy overreach concerns, backlog buildup.
- **Key Features Used:** Privacy-redacted chat evidence inspector, triage queue, enforcement actions, appeal review.
